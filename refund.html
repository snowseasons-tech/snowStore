Monthly plans

Monthly fees are not refunded for part-months. You can cancel at any time and your plan stops at the end of the month you have paid for.

Earlier digital products

If you bought one of our downloadable products, you may request a refund within 14 days of purchase if it does not work as described and we cannot fix it.

How to request a refund

Email info@snowseasonstech.net with your receipt. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method via Stripe.
