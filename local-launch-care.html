What rank tracking measures

After activation, scheduled checks record your domain’s observed organic position within the first 100 results for the agreed search engine, country, language, location and desktop/mobile profile. We agree these settings during setup and keep them consistent for comparisons. Results outside the checked range are labeled “not found in top 100,” not zero. A failed provider request is reported as unavailable, not as a ranking drop.

Monthly reports include measurement dates and settings, observed positions, movement from the previous successful run, coverage gaps and suggested follow-up actions. Local map-pack/geogrid rankings, Google Business Profile management, ads, competitor tracking and guaranteed ranking improvements are not included. Results vary with location, device and search conditions.
