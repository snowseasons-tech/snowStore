### Major enhancements

New modules, integrations, workflows, datasets, major application changes, and redesigns are **not included** in recurring subscriptions. They require a separate written change order or SOW covering scope, acceptance criteria, schedule, estimate, and payment terms **before work begins**.

- Fixed-price projects: ordinarily 50% at approval and 50% upon acceptance, unless the SOW states otherwise.

- Time-and-materials projects: agreed hourly rate, preapproved not-to-exceed budget, and written approval before overruns.

- Maintenance corrects defects in agreed existing functionality; it is not treated as billable enhancement solely because fixing it is complex.

## 5. Subscription Service Boundaries

**Typically included within the agreed scope:** existing software access, routine monitoring, security updates, backup scheduling, correction of defects in supported functionality, and defined content/administration allowances.

**Separately quoted:** new functionality, major upgrades, new data-source collection, significant migrations, extra original content, advertising management, extensive design/video, and incremental infrastructure capacity.

**Client data and software rights:** The client retains applicable rights to its proprietary customer and business data. Snow Seasons Tech may retain ownership of reusable technology only to the extent supported by signed agreements and applicable law. Agreements should define access, export, retention, termination, and third-party data rights.

**AI expenses:** Internal Claude development subscriptions are Snow Seasons Tech operating expenses. Production API consumption, if any, is separately metered or explicitly covered by an agreed allowance; no unlimited usage is implied.
