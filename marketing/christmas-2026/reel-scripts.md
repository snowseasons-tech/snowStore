## Before posting

- Check every product shown is live in the catalog (`api/_lib/catalog.js`) and that the screens match the current version.
- The feature claims in the on-screen text ("reorder alerts", "leads sorted while you sleep", "blog posts without the blank page") are placeholders. Check each against the product before recording, and cut any that don't hold.
- Keep prices off screen. Prices come from the store and change, and the Facebook calendar bans them in posts.
- Use only screen recordings of the owner's own software, with no real customer data.
