# Facebook Reels and Story: December 2026

Two reels and one story to go with the December feed calendar in `posts.md`. Each one repeats a feed post's topic in a vertical format, so they go out the day after that post.

Everything is 1080×1920 in `reels/`. Rebuild with `src/render-reels.sh` (slides live in `src/vertical.html`).

Before you post:
- **Add music in the app.** The MP4s have a silent audio track. Pick a track from Facebook's own music library when you upload; don't add your own audio file unless you hold the rights.
- **Cover image.** Use the matching `-cover.png` (the hook slide) as the reel's cover.
- **Safe zones.** Text stays out of the top 280px, bottom 460px and right 150px, so Facebook's buttons and captions don't cover it. Keep stickers and text you add in the app inside that area too.
- **No prices**, same rule as the feed posts.
