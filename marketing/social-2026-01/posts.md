## Before you post

- **Images** are 1080×1080 in `images/`. Facebook, Instagram and Google all accept square images.
- **Instagram** doesn't make links clickable in captions, so the copy says "link in bio". Put `snowseasonstech.store` in the bio.
- **Google Business Profile** posts have no hashtags. Each one ends with a button label and URL. Pick the button named in the copy (Learn more or Book). Google posts are short, so keep them to the copy below.
- **Blog links** point to the blog index. Swap in article URLs when you publish new articles.
