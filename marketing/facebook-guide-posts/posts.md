## How these follow the guide

- **Length.** Keep the first lines under about 240 characters, because longer posts are cut off behind "See More" (p. 178).
- **Variety.** Mix questions, shout-outs, tips, photos, links and CTAs, and don't post only about yourself (pp. 179–180, Rule #3 p. 39).
- **Human voice.** Write in first person and sign with the admin's first name. Add the admin's photo or bio on a tab if more than one person posts (Rule #4, pp. 40–41).
- **Two-way dialog.** Each post ends with a question or a clear invitation, and the Page should reply to comments (pp. 42, 278).
- **Consistent CTA.** Use the same closing line where possible, so readers learn what to do next (Rule #8, p. 43).
- **Links.** Paste the link into the Publisher, then delete the raw URL so the preview card shows. Keep it only when the link is long or messy (p. 182).
- **Photos.** A photo makes a post take up more room in News Feed. Use one with a post when you can (p. 183).
- **Schedule.** Post on a regular rhythm and keep to it (p. 283). Mon / Wed / Fri is a steady pattern for a small Page.
- **Tagging.** A Page can tag other Pages, but not individuals, in its own posts (p. 281).

## Notes for the admin

- Reply to comments within the day. The book says a quick human reply impresses people (p. 278).
- If posting once a day isn't possible, post three times a week and keep to that rhythm (p. 283).
- Check Insights after two weeks. Repeat the formats that get the most comments and likes (Rule #9, p. 44).
