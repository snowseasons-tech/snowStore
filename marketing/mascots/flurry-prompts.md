# Flurry: image prompts

Flurry is the proposed Christmas mascot for Snow Seasons Tech. The name and tagline are proposals and have not been trademark-checked. Search the name before using it commercially.

**Tagline (proposed):** "Fresh downloads for the season."

## Shared character lock

Use this description in every prompt: a round, friendly snowball character with two stubby arms and two dot eyes with a small smile. It wears a short ice-blue scarf with one amber stripe, and holds a slate-gray gift box with an amber down-arrow on the lid when the pose calls for it. Flat vector logo style, clean even fills, simple slate-gray outlines, no gradients.

**Palette:** ice blue `#a1eaff`, snow white `#f4f4f4`, slate `#46556b`, amber `#ffc46b`.

**Shared avoid line:** any text or letters, extra arms or limbs, gradients, background clutter, a watermark, a Santa hat or red suit, realistic shading.

## 4. Waving (email signature)

A round snowball character with one arm raised in a small friendly wave and the other arm at its side. Two dot eyes with a small smile. Ice-blue scarf with one amber stripe. Head and upper body only, centered, plain white background, generous margin on all sides so it crops cleanly in a small signature.

**Avoid:** the shared avoid line.

## 5. Face only (social avatar)

A round snowball face filling the frame, with two dot eyes and a small smile, and a sliver of ice-blue scarf visible at the bottom edge. No arms and no box. Background is a solid ice-blue `#a1eaff` fill. Square crop, face centered, slightly tight framing.

**Avoid:** the shared avoid line, plus any second character or pattern in the background.

## 6. Confetti, the flaw made visible (holiday banner)

A round snowball character with both arms flung up in excitement, its slate-gray gift box tipped sideways and spilling small amber and ice-blue confetti pieces into the air around it. Two dot eyes wide open with a big grin. Ice-blue scarf with one amber stripe. Wide horizontal framing with the character on the left third, open space on the right for a headline, plain white background.

**Avoid:** the shared avoid line, plus confetti over the character's face, and any readable text in the confetti.

## 7. Thinking (help or FAQ pages)

A round snowball character standing still with one hand raised to its chin, the other arm at its side, and its eyes looking slightly up and to one side. Ice-blue scarf with one amber stripe. Full body, centered, plain white background.

**Avoid:** the shared avoid line.

## 8. Surprised (error pages or a "new release" alert)

A round snowball character with its two dot eyes opened wide into round circles and its mouth a small open "o". Both stubby arms are raised slightly out to its sides, and its slate-gray gift box is tipped a little at its feet, with its amber down-arrow still visible. Ice-blue scarf with one amber stripe. Full body, centered, plain white background, space around the edges.

**Avoid:** the shared avoid line, plus a wide-open mouth larger than the head, and any "!" or other symbols floating over its head.

## 9. Determined (a "get started" or setup-step page)

A round snowball character standing firm with its two dot eyes narrowed into a focused look and a small straight mouth. It holds its slate-gray gift box with the amber down-arrow in front of its body with both arms, and its scarf is pulled up slightly higher on its neck, in ice blue with one amber stripe. Full body, centered, plain white background.

**Avoid:** the shared avoid line, plus any angry expression, clenched fists, or a frown that reads as unhappy rather than focused.

## 10. Character sheet

Put Flurry on a single plain white sheet, shown as a reference lineup for artists and designers. Along the top row, draw three full-body views of the same round snowball character, left to right: front view, three-quarter view, and back view. Each view shows two stubby arms, two dot eyes with a small smile in the front views, and the ice-blue scarf with one amber stripe. In the front view, the slate-gray gift box is held in front of the body with the amber down-arrow on its lid. Along the bottom row, draw five head-and-shoulders close-ups of the same character, left to right: happy, thinking (one hand raised to the chin), surprised (eyes wide, small "o" mouth), determined (narrowed eyes, straight mouth, scarf pulled up), and celebrating (eyes closed in a big grin, arms raised). Keep every view the same size, aligned on a shared baseline, with even spacing and no overlap. Flat vector logo style, clean even fills, simple slate-gray outlines, no gradients, no shading. Plain white background.

**Avoid:** any text or labels, extra arms or limbs, a character that changes size or proportions between views, gradients, background clutter, a watermark, a Santa hat or red suit, realistic shading.

## 11. Cone (variation: pine-cone character)

A friendly pine cone character with a plump, mitten-shaped body. Its body is made of neat rows of overlapping scales in slate gray, each scale tipped in amber. Two stubby arms and two dot eyes with a small smile. It wears an ice-blue scarf with one amber stripe. Flat vector logo style, clean even fills, simple slate-gray outlines, no gradients. Full body, centered, plain white background, space around the edges.

**Avoid:** the shared avoid line, plus a fully brown or natural-coloured pine cone, and any scale pattern that makes the face hard to read.

## 12. Globe (variation: snow-globe creature with a laptop)

A round snow globe creature. Its clear glass dome is a flat ice-blue fill outlined in slate gray, with one simple white highlight stripe on the upper left. Its base is snow white. Inside the dome, a small snowball character with two dot eyes and a small smile holds a tiny slate-gray laptop with an amber screen. A few snowflakes float inside the dome. Flat vector logo style, clean even fills, no gradients, no realistic glass reflections. Full body, centered, plain white background, space around the edges.

**Avoid:** the shared avoid line, plus any readable text or symbols on the laptop screen, and any background outside the dome.

---

## Variation notes

- **Cone** is the option to test if the snowball feels too plain. It keeps the same palette and scarf, so it reads as part of the same family.
- **Globe** is the more tech-forward option. Keep the laptop screen blank, since no readable text is allowed in the images.
