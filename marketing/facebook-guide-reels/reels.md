# Facebook Reels: from the guide posts

Three vertical reels built from the nine drafts in `../facebook-guide-posts/posts.md`. Each one repeats a draft's topic in video form. Nothing is posted yet.

Everything is 1080×1920 in `reels/`. Rebuild with `src/render.sh` (slides live in `src/slides.html`).

| # | Reel | Length | Pairs with | File |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Try before you buy | 9s | Draft 2 (free lessons) | `01-try-before-you-buy.mp4` |
| R2 | Fill in the blank | 10s | Draft 1 (job you'd automate) | `02-fill-in-the-blank.mp4` |
| R3 | The fixes nobody sees | 10s | Draft 5 (behind the scenes) | `03-fixes-nobody-sees.mp4` |

Before you post:
- **Add music in the app.** The MP4s have a silent audio track. Pick a track from Facebook's music library when you upload.
- **Cover image.** Use the matching `-cover.png` as the reel's cover.
- **Safe zones.** Text stays out of the top 280px, bottom 460px and right 150px, so Facebook's buttons and captions don't cover it.
- **R3 examples are generic.** The three "fixes" on screen are examples, not real changes. Swap in real ones before posting, or cut the slide.
- **No prices**, same rule as the feed posts.
