Frequently asked questions

How do I get my download?+

Right after checkout you land on your order page with a download button, and we email you a link to it. Customers with an account can also sign in to the customer portal to download the apps they own.

Do I own the app forever?+

Yes. A license is a one-time purchase and the version you buy keeps working forever. The first year of updates and support is included.

What happens when updates & support expire?+

Nothing stops working. Renew any time to get new versions and support again. You can turn auto-renewal on or off for each app.

Can I buy for my whole team?+

Yes. Buy one license per person who uses the app. Buying 5 or more licenses of an app gets you 15% off.
