---
title: "Founder's Early Access Membership"
---

Subscription access to the eligible Early Access downloads while the membership is active.

## Getting started

1. Sign in to the customer portal with the account used for your membership.
2. Choose a download that is included in the membership and follow the instructions packaged with it.
3. Check your membership status and account email if a download you expect is missing.
