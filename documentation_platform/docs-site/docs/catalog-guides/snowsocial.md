---
title: "SnowSocial — Social Content Planner"
---

Keep your brand active with up to 12 social posts per month, adapted captions and simple branded graphics. Review and approve content before scheduled publishing to up to five connected profiles, with monthly performance reporting.

## Getting started

1. Prepare your brand assets, publishing profiles and a list of topics for the month.
2. Review the proposed captions and graphics, then approve the items you want scheduled.
3. Use the monthly report to review performance and plan the next batch.
