This product is retired. This guide is for customers who already have it.

A self-hosted social media manager for agencies: client workspaces, team roles, an AI writer, approvals, a content calendar and scheduled publishing to Facebook, Instagram and Google Business Profile.

## Getting started

1. Use the deployment instructions packaged with your existing release.
2. Create a workspace, assign team roles and test the draft and approval flow before connecting accounts.
3. Preview the schedule and verify the target account and time zone before publishing.
