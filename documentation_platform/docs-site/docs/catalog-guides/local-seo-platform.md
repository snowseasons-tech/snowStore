---
title: "Local Launch — Local SEO Website Platform"
---

Build and export a local-service business website with editable pages, content SEO checks, a Business Profile checklist and manual keyword reports. Downloadable software; hosting is separate.

## Getting started

1. Unzip Local Launch and follow the included setup instructions.
2. Create a site and fill in the business name, service area, contact details and service pages.
3. Run the content checks, preview the site, then export it for your hosting provider.
