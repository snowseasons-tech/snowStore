title: "GBP Analytics Dashboard"
---

Turn Google Business Profile CSV reports into interactive charts: Search and Maps visibility, calls, website clicks and direction requests, compared across locations. Not affiliated with Google.

## Getting started

1. Export performance CSVs from Google Business Profile.
2. Open the dashboard in your browser and load the CSVs for the locations you want to compare.
3. Check the date ranges before comparing Search and Maps visibility, calls, website clicks or directions.
