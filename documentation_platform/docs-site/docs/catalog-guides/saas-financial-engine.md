---
title: "SaaS Financial Projection Engine"
---

A 5-year SaaS financial model in one Excel workbook: MRR/ARR, unit economics, cohorts, headcount, cap table and scenarios. Works in Excel, Google Sheets and LibreOffice.

## Getting started

1. Make a copy of the workbook for your forecast.
2. Enter your starting assumptions for pricing, customers, churn and headcount.
3. Compare scenarios and review how changes affect recurring revenue and cash. Keep the original workbook as a reference.
