This product is retired. This guide is for customers who already have it.

Role scorecards, a 142-question interview bank, global salary bands, an offer letter generator and a 30-60-90 onboarding plan. Excel, PDF and Word.

## Getting started

1. Open the scorecard and choose the role you are hiring for.
2. Pick interview questions tied to the role and agree on scoring with the interview team.
3. Review any supplied salary benchmarks against current market data before making an offer.
