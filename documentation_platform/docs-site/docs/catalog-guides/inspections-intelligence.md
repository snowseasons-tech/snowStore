Fleet inspections and geofence event monitoring.

## Getting started

1. Open the dotTracker preview and select a sample fleet or vehicle.
2. Replay the sample route and compare geofence events with the vehicle’s movement.
3. Ask your account contact about live fleet onboarding and the data required for your plan.
