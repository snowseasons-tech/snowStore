# Welcome to our new Documentation Center!

We are thrilled to announce the launch of our centralized **Documentation Platform**. As we expand our suite of SaaS tools—from **CDOT Stations** to **Lead Intelligence**—we wanted to ensure that our customers have a world-class experience accessing the information they need.

## What's New?

Our new system isn't just a collection of pages; it's a **Docs-as-Code** pipeline. This means:

- **Always Up-to-Date**: Documentation is generated directly from our product registries. When a feature is added to the software, it's added to the docs.
- **Professional PDFs**: Need a hard copy? Every guide now comes with a professional, branded PDF download.
- **Secure Access**: Via our Customer Portal, you only see the documentation for the products you are subscribed to.

## How to get started

1. Visit our [Documentation Center](/docs/intro).
2. Sign in to the [Customer Portal](https://portal.snowseasonstech.net) to access your private product sheets.
3. Explore the a-z of our software capabilities.

We are committed to transparency and clarity. If there's something missing from our guides, let us know through the support ticket system!
