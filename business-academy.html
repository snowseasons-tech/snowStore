Your execution sequence

Online business launch roadmap

Use this sequence as a practical project plan. The work is iterative: revisit earlier decisions when customer evidence or operating constraints change.

01Validate the opportunity — customer, problem, competition, and offer.
02Set the business foundation — structure, records, policies, and professional advice.
03Build the customer experience — domain, website, content, accessibility, and hosting.
04Design operations — support, fulfillment, vendors, inventory, and data.
05Protect the business — accounts, payment flows, backups, incident response, and continuity.
06Acquire and convert customers — positioning, content, search, referrals, and measurement.
07Launch commerce — catalog, pricing, checkout, payment, delivery, returns, and reconciliation.
08Improve and expand — retention, new channels, niche opportunities, and sustainable growth.
Decision gate: do not scale traffic or product complexity until the core customer journey works reliably from discovery through payment, delivery, and support.
