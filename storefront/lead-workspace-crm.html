HUBSPOT · REVIEWED CONTACT DELIVERY

Review first. Deliver with confidence.

Create or update contacts by email. Only mapped, non-empty fields are sent. Existing contacts keep their unmapped fields.

Checking CRM access…

1. Connect HubSpot

Use a service key from your intended HubSpot test account with contact read and write scopes. The key is encrypted before storage and never returned to this page.

2. Select and map

Select up to 10 leads. Each needs a distinct contact email.

Choose which lead field supplies each HubSpot value. Contact email is required. Unmapped or empty values are left unchanged in HubSpot.

3. Review and send

Prepare a preview to see the exact fields that will be sent.

Sending updates contacts that already match the email. This action does not send outreach messages. Existing HubSpot automations remain controlled by your HubSpot account.

Delivery results

Succeeded rows are skipped on retry. Retryable rows wait until the displayed time; attempts are limited to three per review. Reviews expire after 15 minutes.
