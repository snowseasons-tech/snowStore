Prices are proposed launch hypotheses, not researched market benchmarks. Target adults who own small businesses and have no coding experience. Written walkthroughs and heavily commented code are the core product. No video, coaching, hosting or production services are included.

## Product descriptions
Free lesson: Business service page starter. Follow a beginner walkthrough, edit a commented starter, compare a completed example and check your answers. Allow 45–60 minutes with a computer, plain-text editor and browser.

Paid pack: Complete service information page. Work through four sessions using practical business examples, guided exercises, completed code, answers and a quick reference. Allow 2–3 hours and complete the free starter first. All business names and prices are fictional.

## Eight lesson roadmap
Semantic structure; service tables and navigation; image accessibility; contact form markup; metadata; multi-page navigation; publishing fundamentals; capstone service website.

The first two lesson products are included. The other six are planned, not completed downloads. Publish accurate contents and prerequisites rather than selling the roadmap as finished material.

## Before release
Pilot with three beginners. Check both starter and solution pages in current Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge; test narrow layouts and keyboard use. Review TESTING.md for actual completed checks. Choose support contact, refund policy and license before listing. For the free listing package free_starter and SETUP.md. For the paid listing package paid_pack, SETUP.md and COMMAND_CHEAT_SHEET.md. Keep seller notes private. Test checkout and download contents before publishing.
