Prices are proposed launch hypotheses, not researched market benchmarks. Target adults who own small businesses and have no coding experience. Written walkthroughs and heavily commented code are the core product. No video, coaching, hosting or production services are included.

## Product descriptions
Free lesson: Click to show a service rate. Follow a beginner walkthrough, edit a commented starter, compare a completed example and check your answers. Allow 45–60 minutes with a computer, plain-text editor and browser.

Paid pack: Interactive quote calculator. Work through four sessions using practical business examples, guided exercises, completed code, answers and a quick reference. Allow 2–3 hours and complete the free starter first. All business names and prices are fictional.

## Eight lesson roadmap
Events and text updates; validated estimate calculator; arrays and service selection; filtering lists; JSON and local storage; asynchronous requests; debugging and tests; capstone business tool.

The first two lesson products are included. The other six are planned, not completed downloads. Publish accurate contents and prerequisites rather than selling the roadmap as finished material.

## Before release
Pilot with three beginners. Check both starter and solution pages in current Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge; test narrow layouts and keyboard use. Review TESTING.md for actual completed checks. Choose support contact, refund policy and license before listing.
