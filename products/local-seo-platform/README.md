# Local Launch — Local SEO Website Platform
Version 1.0.0 | Snow Seasons Tech

## Start here
Unzip the complete package, keep the files together, and open index.html in a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari browser. No installation, account, API key or subscription is required. The builder runs on your device. Internet is needed for external Google links and your eventual hosting/form provider, not for generating files.

1. Open Business profile. Enter your real name, contact details, hours and HTTPS domain.
2. Enter one service per line as `Service name | Specific description`.
3. Enter genuine service areas as `Location | Useful local details`. An area without details does not generate a page.
4. Add authorized reviews as `Exact review | Attribution | Source and context` if desired.
5. Choose a template and brand color, then save.
6. Review each page in Website builder. Edit titles, descriptions, main headings and content. Save each edit. Use Phone preview to check a narrow layout.
7. Work through SEO & launch. The score covers generated content only, not live site performance or search rankings.
8. Export website. Extract its ZIP, review LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.txt and upload the contents to a static hosting provider. Your homepage is index.html. Keep all pages at the same directory level.
9. Configure your domain, HTTPS and optional form endpoint with their providers. Test the deployed site and submit sitemap.xml to Google Search Console.
10. Complete the manual Business Profile checklist, record keyword observations with their source, and save a monthly snapshot. Export a report and use your browser's Print command for a PDF.

## Limits and honest expectations
This is a downloadable builder, not a hosted SaaS subscription. Hosting, domain registration, contact-form delivery, account authentication, scheduled scans, automated ranking checks, Google API integration, live crawling, analytics and call tracking are not included. No guaranteed search rankings. No automatic review sourcing or fabricated reviews. Photos/media editing is not included in v1.

The score starts at 100 and deducts 12 for each High, 4 for each Medium and 1 for each Low finding, with a floor of zero. Character counts are editing heuristics. A score is not an SEO certification or a prediction of traffic.

Project data stays in your browser's local storage; it is not sent to Snow Seasons Tech. File URLs and private-mode storage behavior vary by browser. Export a project backup after each session. Clearing browser storage can erase your workspace. Imports replace the active workspace. Review text edits after renaming or reordering services because page identifiers may change.

Generated form submissions go to the HTTPS endpoint you configure. Choose a provider, configure its allowed domains and retention/privacy policy, and test delivery yourself. If no endpoint is supplied, the contact page uses your email link. Set an accurate public contact method before publishing.

## Troubleshooting
- Blank or unstyled: extract the ZIP first and keep index.html, engine.js, app.js and style.css together.
- Changes missing: save the business form or page before switching views; import your latest backup.
- Export blocked: allow downloads in your browser. The ZIP is generated locally.
- Form doesn't deliver: verify the provider endpoint, allowed origin and provider inbox; this product does not supply a mail server.
- No area page: add genuine local details after `|` for that location.
- Bad canonical/sitemap: set the final HTTPS URL, including any subdirectory, then export again.
